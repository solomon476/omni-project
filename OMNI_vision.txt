1. WHAT IS OMNI?
Omni is an intelligent, independent, and connected human safety ecosystem designed to protect, support, monitor, and assist individuals during emergencies, vulnerable situations, health-related concerns, and emotional distress.
Omni combines software systems, wearable technology, sensors, AI-assisted intelligence, emergency communication systems, and connected hardware infrastructure to create a proactive safety and wellbeing platform.
Unlike traditional emergency systems that rely entirely on manual interaction, Omni is envisioned to operate intelligently and independently when necessary.
The system is being designed to:
• understand human conditions
• monitor distress indicators
• provide emotional support
• coordinate emergency workflows
• assist users even when they cannot ask for help themselves
2. THE CORE PURPOSE OF OMNI
To ensure that people can still receive protection, support, communication, and assistance during moments of danger, vulnerability, distress, or incapacity.
3. THE PROBLEM OMNI SOLVES
Most current emergency systems:
• rely entirely on manual activation
• only react after emergencies happen
• lack intelligent interpretation
• do not monitor wellbeing continuously
• cannot assist unconscious individuals
• do not integrate emotional support
4. OMNI’S CORE VISION
Omni is envisioned as an intelligent safety, wellbeing, monitoring, and emergency response ecosystem capable of understanding human conditions and assisting proactively during vulnerable situations.
